OUR PROJECTS

KAZZ AND BIBBLE: WANNABE WIZARDS

Kazz and Bibble are not real wizards. They failed every magic class, dropped out of school, and live in a moldy hill house with nothing to their names but ego and desperation. After accidentally completing a real wizarding job without using magic, they start a business offering magical services they are wildly unqualified for.

›

I'M SCARED OF ALOTTA STUFF

A supernatural comedy-horror series about a group of teens forced to face their fears when godlike beings turn anxiety, trauma, and insecurity into real monsters. It's funny, messy, and emotional. Showing that bravery isn't about being fearless, but about facing what scares you anyway.

›

HOME SWEET HOME

Socks, Velcra and Patches never belonged on their planets. The three misfits came to Earth, the armpit of the universe, to hide from their authorities but they soon realize that life on Earth isn't so easy either when the AAH organization discovers their true identities…

›

LEARNING WITH PIBBY: ARMAGEDDON

A NON-PROFIT, FANMADE story based off the canceled Learning With Pibby: Apocalypse trailer! A preschool cartoon character stranded in corrupted worlds must decide to save the universe as a hero, or risk the Darkness consuming them first.
