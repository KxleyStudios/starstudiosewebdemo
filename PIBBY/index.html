LEARNING WITH PIBBY:
ARMAGEDDON

A fanmade continuation of the legendary canceled Pibby trailer. A preschool cartoon character trapped in corrupted worlds must choose: become the hero the universe needs, or let the Darkness take everything.

In Development Fan Project Non-Profit

FULL SYNOPSIS

A preschool cartoon character, Pibby, is stranded alone in a universe being consumed by a glitch-like force called the Darkness. Forced to cross through corrupted cartoon worlds, she must survive the infected versions of characters she once called friendly, gather allies, and make an impossible choice: use the power of the Darkness herself and risk becoming what she's fighting, or find another way to save everything she's ever known.
